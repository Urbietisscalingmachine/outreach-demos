⚠️ Electrical Hazards Are Real

Why Safety Matters
In Every Job

Electrical failures are the #2 cause of house fires in the US. We don't cut corners - ever.

🔥

Overloaded Panels Cause Fires

An outdated 100-amp panel can't handle today's modern appliances. Overloaded circuits arc and spark - often inside your walls where you can't see it. That's how house fires start.

⚡ 51,000 home electrical fires per year in the US
💀

DIY Wiring Kills

Improperly wired outlets and switches create invisible shock hazards. One reversed hot/neutral wire can energize an entire appliance chassis. We inspect what "the last guy" left behind.

⚡ 400+ electrocution deaths in US homes each year
🚗

EV Chargers Need Dedicated Circuits

Plugging your EV into a standard outlet draws continuous current your wiring wasn't designed for. Overheated wires don't just trip breakers - they melt insulation and smolder for hours.

⚡ Level 2 install requires proper 240V dedicated line
